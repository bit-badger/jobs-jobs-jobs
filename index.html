Jobs, Jobs, Jobs (…and Jobs – Let’s Vote for Jobs!)

For four years, this site enabled citizens of Gitmo Nation to assist one another in finding employment. While its development was driven by popular demand, the privacy required to keep such a system within the community (and away from either headhunters or job spammers) was the largest driver behind the site's ongoing low utilization. The site was decommissioned in May 2025.

While this site is no longer active, No Agenda is still going strong. It’s a click away; check out The Best Podcast in the Universe (that’s true!) and find out what you’re missing.
